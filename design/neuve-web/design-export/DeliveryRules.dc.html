Motion: moving the dial

One height movement per change. The blocks that leave fade out first, last block first and 27.5ms apart, while the height holds. Once the last of them is at 30% opacity, the height changes once, from the old total to the new one, on a curve that never overshoots. Then the arriving blocks fade in with a small rise, in reading order and 27.5ms apart, from 75% of that movement, so at Guided the suggested review still arrives after its reasons. The unit’s title, and on a joined unit each change’s title, stay still.

The “Done when” card leaves verdict first, one block at a time, then the claims; the height moves once when its last block is at 30%; Before and After, the terms, toolbar and diff fade in from 75% of the move. The titles never move in either direction, so the reader keeps one fixed point.

Scroll anchoringIf the reader is down in the diff, the line they’re reading stays put while the stack changes height above it.
Rapid changesA quick return reverses from where things are: blocks still on screen come straight back, and a moving height turns toward the new total. No queue, no replay.
Reduced motionThe thumb snaps; the height snaps once and blocks fade in place over the reduced 150ms, together, with no move, rise or stagger.
