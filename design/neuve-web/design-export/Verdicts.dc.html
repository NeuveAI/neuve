Comment pins · state around the avatar

A comment pin is its author's avatar. Hover, focus and open draw a ring around it in the same colours for everyone. On an agent the ring sits just outside the dashed edge, and the antenna rises to clear it. Resolved is quiet: a person keeps a thin ring, an agent goes back to its dashed edge, and both get a check beside them.

Checkpoints: an unmet one (Missing, Out of date or Conflicting) sits under the item it fails, with its state, what it asks for, Records' note and where it sits; an item whose checkpoints are all met looks as before. Every other checkpoint is one count row at the foot, a native disclosure grouped by item, and the suggested review counts the unmet ones. The agent's word never changes with them.

No ticket: the harness climbs from the change itself to commit history, earlier reviews, the blast radius and docs until the intent is stated and every item cites a fact. No ticket id; the evidence comes mostly from Source and History.
