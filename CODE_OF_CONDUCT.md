# Code of conduct

Neuve follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). It applies in the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions, and anywhere someone represents the project.

In short: be kind and specific, assume good intent, critique the work and not the person, and leave room for people who are new or who disagree.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour is a problem, email the maintainers at [support@neuve.ai](mailto:support@neuve.ai). Reports are read only by the maintainers and kept confidential. If a maintainer is involved, say so, and they will not handle the report.

Maintainers decide on consequences using the Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines), from a private warning to a permanent ban.
